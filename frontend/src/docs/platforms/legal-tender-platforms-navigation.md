# Legal Tender And Notice Platforms - Morocco, France, EU

Checked: 2026-08-16

Purpose: identify platforms that overlap with a public tender/legal notice aggregation product, then document how a user reaches opportunities, legal notices, documents, alerts, or submission areas.

This is a practical market/navigation map, not a legal-source ranking. Official portals remain the authoritative source when a tender, award, legal notice, or company filing is disputed.

## Morocco Platforms

### Portail Marocain des Marches Publics

- URL: <https://www.marchespublics.gov.ma/>
- Operator: Tresorerie Generale du Royaume.
- Type: official public procurement portal.
- What it offers: consultations, tender notices, award notices, procurement plans, reports, excluded companies, DCE/document access, bidder account services.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open the homepage.
  2. Enter the enterprise/supplier area or use the public search for consultations.
  3. Filter by object/keyword, buyer, category, procedure, region, publication date, and deadline where available.
  4. Open a consultation result.
  5. Review buyer, object, category, place of execution, estimate/caution when published, deadline, and attached documents.
  6. Download the DCE or continue to authenticated enterprise services for questions, withdrawal tracking, and submission.
- Overlap with us: primary official source for Moroccan tenders. Our value can be normalization, faster scanning, enrichment, alerts, and guidance.
- Notes: use this as the canonical validation source for Morocco public procurement.

### ANP Portail Fournisseurs

- URL: <https://www.anp.org.ma/> and supplier/consultation sections linked from the ANP site.
- Operator: Agence Nationale des Ports.
- Type: public buyer portal.
- What it offers: port-sector tenders, supplier information, procurement announcements, and sometimes dedicated access to DCE or consultation files.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open the ANP website.
  2. Navigate to supplier, achats, appels d'offres, or marches sections.
  3. Select current consultations or notices.
  4. Filter or scan by publication date, deadline, port/site, and subject.
  5. Open the notice to retrieve file references, requirements, and document download/submission instructions.
- Overlap with us: important buyer-specific source for port and infrastructure opportunities.
- Notes: buyer portals are useful for completeness checks against the national portal.

### ONCF Appels d'offres

- URL: <https://www.oncf.ma/>
- Operator: Office National des Chemins de Fer.
- Type: public buyer portal.
- What it offers: rail-sector procurement notices, consultation documents, amendments, and results.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open the ONCF site.
  2. Navigate to enterprise/supplier/procurement sections, usually labelled appels d'offres or achats.
  3. Open the list of current tenders.
  4. Scan by reference, subject, publication date, deadline, and category.
  5. Open the notice page or file link for consultation details and withdrawal/submission instructions.
- Overlap with us: high-value vertical source for transport, works, services, and supplies.
- Notes: useful as a sector-specific benchmark for document organization and tender status labels.

### ONDA Appels d'offres

- URL: <https://www.onda.ma/>
- Operator: Office National des Aeroports.
- Type: public buyer portal.
- What it offers: airport-sector calls for tenders, consultation details, addenda, and buyer procurement news.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open the ONDA website.
  2. Navigate to appels d'offres, achats, or fournisseur pages.
  3. Select current tenders.
  4. Review reference, object, airport/location, submission deadline, and document links.
  5. Follow the notice instructions for file withdrawal and submission.
- Overlap with us: sector-specific opportunity feed, especially for construction, maintenance, safety, IT, and airport services.
- Notes: buyer portals often expose updates or addenda that aggregators should track.

### MASEN eTendering

## France And EU Official Platforms

### Marches Publics France - PLACE

- URL: <https://www.marches-publics.gouv.fr/entreprise>
- Operator: French State purchasing platform.
- Type: official State buyer platform/profile buyer.
- What it offers: public tender search, consultation pages, DCE download, questions, electronic submission, user account area.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open the enterprise page.
  2. Go to the consultation search/listing area.
  3. Search by keyword, reference, buyer, procedure, place of execution, CPV, publication date, or deadline.
  4. Open a consultation.
  5. Review avis, DCE, consultation rules, deadlines, buyer contact, Q&A, and amendments.
  6. Create/sign in to an enterprise account to ask questions, follow the consultation, or submit an offer.
- Overlap with us: best benchmark for official tender journey and authenticated supplier workflows.
- Notes: strong reference for saved searches, DCE workflow, question management, and bid submission UX.

### BOAMP

- URL: <https://www.boamp.fr/>
- Operator: Direction de l'information legale et administrative.
- Type: official public procurement notice publication.
- What it offers: French public tender notices, award notices, rectifications, search, saved watch features, and notice exports.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open BOAMP.
  2. Use the search for avis.
  3. Filter by keyword, buyer, department, CPV, procedure, notice type, publication date, and response deadline.
  4. Open the notice detail.
  5. Review buyer, object, lot structure, deadline, procedure, links to buyer profile/DCE, and notice history.
  6. Use alert/watch options if logged in or available.
- Overlap with us: official source for French notices, useful benchmark for search filters and notice structure.
- Notes: BOAMP often links out to buyer profiles for DCE and submission.

### TED - Tenders Electronic Daily

- URL: <https://ted.europa.eu/>
- Operator: Publications Office of the European Union.
- Type: official EU procurement notice platform.
- What it offers: EU-wide contract notices, prior information notices, award notices, notices by CPV/country/buyer, advanced search, and exports.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open TED.
  2. Use Search or Advanced Search.
  3. Filter by country, buyer, CPV code, notice type, publication date, deadline, value, language, and procedure.
  4. Open a notice.
  5. Review procedure data, lots, CPV, place of performance, deadlines, buyer contacts, and procurement document links.
  6. Export, save, or create alerts where available.
- Overlap with us: benchmark for multilingual, standardized procurement data at scale.
- Notes: useful for modeling data fields and advanced filter taxonomy.

## France Legal Notice Platforms

### PPLE - Portail De La Publicite Legale Des Entreprises

- URL: <https://www.pple.fr/>
- Type: official entry point for French company legal publicity.
- What it offers: access to company legal-publicity data from BODACC, legal-announcement newspapers, and registry-related sources.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open PPLE.
  2. Search by company name, SIREN/SIRET, executive name, or legal notice terms.
  3. Filter or select source families such as BODACC, annonces legales, or registry publications when available.
  4. Open the result to reach the underlying legal-publicity record.
  5. Follow links to the official source for full details or documents.
- Overlap with us: model for cross-source legal notice aggregation.
- Notes: useful benchmark for company-centric legal watch.

### BODACC

- URL: <https://www.bodacc.fr/>
- Operator: Direction de l'information legale et administrative.
- Type: official civil and commercial announcements bulletin.
- What it offers: notices for company creation, sale/transfer, collective proceedings, modifications, cancellations, and related legal events.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open BODACC.
  2. Use the search for annonces.
  3. Search by company name, SIREN, department, date, event type, or bulletin reference.
  4. Open the notice.
  5. Review the legal event, company identifiers, court/registry references, dates, and publication data.
  6. Save the notice reference or PDF where available.
- Overlap with us: benchmark for legal event taxonomy and company lifecycle monitoring.
- Notes: strong source for supplier risk signals and business intelligence.

### Actulegales

- URL: <https://www.actulegales.fr/>
- Type: legal notice search and aggregation platform.
- What it offers: search across legal notices published in authorized newspapers and company-related announcements.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open Actulegales.
  2. Use company or notice search.
  3. Filter by department, publication date, company name, SIREN, or notice category where available.
  4. Open the notice preview/detail.
  5. Use source references to validate the publication.
- Overlap with us: private/legal-publicity aggregator model.
- Notes: useful benchmark for legal notices as searchable structured records.

### Infogreffe

- URL: <https://www.infogreffe.fr/>
- Type: commercial court registry platform.
- What it offers: company registry data, extracts, filings, annual accounts, proceedings, and registry documents.
- Navigation itinerary:
  1. Open Infogreffe.
  2. Search by company name, SIREN/SIRET, or executive.
  3. Open the company sheet.
  4. Review identity, registry, filings, accounts, and available official documents.
  5. Purchase/download official extracts or documents when required.
- Overlap with us: company enrichment and legal/commercial due-diligence layer.
- Notes: not a tender platform, but strategically relevant for supplier/buyer verification.

## Feature Ideas To Borrow

- Source authority labels: official, buyer profile, private aggregator, legal registry.
- Field confidence: highlight data taken from the official notice versus inferred/enriched fields.
- Navigation links per tender: official notice, DCE/buyer profile, questions/submission, award notice, related buyer page.
- Saved searches: keyword + region + sector + buyer + deadline window.
- Legal/company enrichment: link tenders to company registry records, legal notices, and risk events.
- Update tracking: addenda, deadline changes, rectifications, award updates, and cancelled procedures.
- Alert levels: urgent deadline, new tender, matching sector, buyer follow-up, legal event.
- Country/portal normalization: map CPV/categories, regions, buyers, procedure types, and legal-event types into a common schema.
